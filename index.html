Semigroup orbits

A semigroup of matrices acting on a starting vector (x, y). With L = (1 1; 0 1) and R = (1 0; 1 1), ⟨L, R⁴⟩ is Rickards–Stange's Ψ₁ and ⟨L⁴, R⁴⟩ is Ψ₂; Ψ is their full Kronecker-preserving semigroup (Lemma 4.1). The lab lists every numerator or denominator up to N and sorts the misses as above.

Markov and Lagrange

The dashed line above your number is a hyperbolic geodesic. Its Lagrange value L = limsup 1/(q²·|x − p/q|) measures how deep it keeps cutting into circles. Hurwitz's √5 is the bottom of the spectrum.

Wiener's attack on RSA

If the private exponent d is below about N^¼/3, then k/d is a convergent of e/N. The key is one of the circles on the trail.

Rademacher's path

Rademacher's exact formula for the partition numbers p(n) comes from integrating along the tops of the Ford circles of order N. Each denominator k contributes one term.

Area check

Total area of the circles for fractions in (0, 1], up to the current max denominator (400 when it's set to ∞). As q → ∞ it tends to (π/4)·ζ(3)/ζ(4) ≈ 0.8723, so about 13% of the unit square under the curve stays uncovered forever.

Mediants

Between two tangent circles a/b and c/d, the biggest circle squeezed into the gap is always the mediant (a+c)/(b+d). Select a circle and its two Farey parents are the circles it nests between.

Hurwitz

Every irrational x has infinitely many p/q with |x − p/q| < 1/(√5·q²), and √5 is the best constant. The golden ratio is the worst case, which is why its convergents crawl in so slowly.
